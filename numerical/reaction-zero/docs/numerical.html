What is being computed

Matrix states, rather than scalar illustrations.

Mass is a PSD 2 × 2 matrix in each cell. Flux and reaction are full matrices; their symmetric parts enter the continuity equation. Ellipse axes encode square roots of mass eigenvalues, while the profile and heatmap encode trace density. Tiny ellipse axes have a 0.7-pixel visibility floor; their area is not a mass scale.

How to interpret the evidence

Finite grids illustrate a continuous theorem.

The dashed balanced reference uses the same finite-volume mesh. A separate reaction-only case has the continuous value 1.2 / ε²; time refinement measures its discretization error. Kernel verification applies to the continuous theorem; solver tolerances are numerical diagnostics.

Display between time nodes uses linear interpolation of computed mass matrices. Flux and reaction are piecewise constant on time intervals.

Model, solver residuals and reproducibility

Unit time and space intervals; entrywise matrix divergence; L = I₂. Time-midpoint mass and arithmetic face interpolation define the finite-volume quadratic perspectives.

∂ₜG + ∂ₓ sym Q = sym R

Each transport/reaction perspective is represented by a 4 × 4 PSD Schur-complement epigraph. The reaction block uses R H⁻¹ with the multiplication order retained. Nodal mass matrices are PSD, and both boundary fluxes are zero.
